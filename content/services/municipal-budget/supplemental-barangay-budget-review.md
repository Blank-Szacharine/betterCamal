# Supplemental Barangay Budget Review

The Municipal Budget Office reviews supplemental budgets submitted by Camalaniugan's barangays.

## Checklist

- Transmittal letter — Office of the Sangguniang Barangay.
- Appropriation ordinance — Barangay Secretary and Punong Barangay.
- Statement of funding sources — Barangay Treasurer, Municipal Accountant, and Punong Barangay.
- Approved supplemental AIP — Office of the Sangguniang Barangay.

Confirm the current checklist and submission schedule with the Municipal Budget Office before filing.
