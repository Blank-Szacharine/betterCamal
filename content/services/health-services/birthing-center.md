# Birthing Center - Camalaniugan

The Municipal Health Office's Birthing Center provides 24/7 service for deliveries and maternity support.

## Before visiting

- Contact the Municipal Health Office or your midwife for current admission and delivery arrangements.
- Bring your maternal health record, identification, and any documents requested by the health worker.
- Seek urgent medical assistance immediately for emergencies or warning signs during pregnancy.

Delivery eligibility and referral arrangements are assessed by health professionals. Confirm the current service arrangements with the Municipal Health Office.

## Office
