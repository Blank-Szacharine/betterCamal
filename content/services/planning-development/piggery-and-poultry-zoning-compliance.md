# Zoning Compliance for Piggery and Poultry

The Municipal Planning and Development Office publishes a separate zoning-compliance checklist for piggery and poultry projects.

## Basic requirements

Submit three copies of the following, unless the office advises otherwise:

1. Duly completed and notarized application form.
2. Certified true copy of title and tax declaration; if the lot is not in the applicant's name, a notarized deed of sale or contract of lease.
3. Vicinity map within a 200-meter radius, showing the project location and surrounding land uses.
4. Site development plan with lot dimensions and the proposed structures.
5. Environmental Clearance Certificate or Certificate of Non-Compliance from DENR.
6. Site clearance from the Local Health Office or Municipal Veterinarian, as applicable.
7. Affidavit of no objection from residents or adjacent lot owners within a 100-meter radius.
8. Barangay Council resolution endorsing the project, with meeting minutes.
9. MAO certification that the site is not feasible for crop production, where required for an agricultural zone.
10. Bill of materials, cost estimates, and one set of technical plans.
11. Authorization for the person allowed to follow up the clearance.
12. Zoning fee.

Confirm the current requirements and fees with the MPDO before preparing an application.
