# Laboratory Program - Blood Chemistry - Camalaniugan

The Blood Chemistry Laboratory Program provides testing requested for clinical assessment, monitoring, or follow-up care.

## Who this is for

Clients with a health worker request or referral for blood chemistry testing.

## What to prepare

- Laboratory request or referral, when applicable
- Valid identification, when available
- Previous results and current medicines
- Follow the staff's instructions about fasting or other preparation

## What to expect

1. Present the request and register with the laboratory staff.
2. Follow the preparation and specimen collection instructions.
3. Ask when and where the result will be released and what follow-up is needed.

The required preparation, fee, and release time can vary by test. Confirm the current details with the Municipal Health Office.

## Office

**Municipal Health Office, Municipality of Camalaniugan**

This service appears in the Citizen's Charter under Laboratory Program - Blood Chemistry, page 49.
