# Business or Mayor's Permit - Camalaniugan

Apply for a new, renewal, temporary, or seasonal business permit.

Prepare barangay clearance, DTI/SEC/CDA registration, health and sanitary clearance, market clearance when applicable, zoning clearance, ecological solid waste clearance, building or occupancy permit, and fire safety inspection certificate. File the application, complete assessment and payment, then present the receipt for release.

**Fees:** Based on Ordinance No. 2011-04. **Office:** BPLO. **Charter:** pages 16-17.
