# Zoning Compliance for Cell Sites

The Municipal Planning and Development Office publishes a zoning-compliance checklist for telecommunications cell-site projects.

## Basic requirements

Submit three copies of the following, unless the office advises otherwise:

1. Duly completed and notarized application form.
2. Certified true copy of title and tax declaration; if the site is not in the applicant's name, a notarized deed of sale or contract of lease.
3. Vicinity map within a 200-meter radius, showing the project location and surrounding land uses.
4. Site development plan with lot dimensions and proposed structures.
5. NTC provisional authority or certificate of registration to provide telecommunications services.
6. Environmental Clearance Certificate from DENR.
7. Radiation-protection evaluation report from the Department of Health Radiation Service.
8. Written consent or certificate of no objection from residents within a 100-meter radius.
9. Barangay Council resolution endorsing the project, with meeting minutes.
10. Bill of materials, cost estimates, and one set of technical plans.
11. Authorization for the person allowed to follow up the clearance.
12. Zoning fee.

Confirm the current requirements and fees with the MPDO before preparing an application.
