# Laboratory Services - Camalaniugan

The Municipal Health Office provides routine and blood-chemistry laboratory examinations. The published service slide identifies the medical technologist as the responsible laboratory staff member.

## Available examinations

- Routine examinations: CBC, CBC with platelet count, platelet count, hemoglobin/hematocrit, WBC, urinalysis, fecalysis, BSMP, blood typing, sputum exam, and Gram staining.
- Blood chemistry: fasting blood sugar, total cholesterol, total triglycerides, HDL, LDL, blood uric acid, creatinine, and HbA1c.

## What to prepare

1. Bring the laboratory request or referral, if applicable.
2. Ask the health worker whether fasting or another preparation is needed for your test.
3. Confirm the current schedule, fee, and result-release time before visiting.

The original image lists rates that may no longer be current, so they are not republished here. The slide states that health-indigent Camalaniugan residents may receive free routine examinations and that non-PhilHealth clients may be charged. Please confirm eligibility and fees directly with the Municipal Health Office.
