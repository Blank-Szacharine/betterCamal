# Mayor's Permit for MTOP - Camalaniugan

Apply for the Motorized Tricycle Operator's Permit before operating a tricycle.

## Prepare

Bring the MTOP franchise from the Sangguniang Bayan Office and the official receipt from the Treasury Office. Submit the documents for checking, then receive the permit.

**Fee:** P150. **Processing:** 5 minutes review plus 3 minutes release. **Office:** Mayor's Office. **Charter:** page 8.
