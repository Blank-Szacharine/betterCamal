# Ecological Solid Waste Management Clearance - Camalaniugan

This clearance may be required as part of an applicable business permit application.

Ask the Business Permit and Licensing Office whether it applies to your business, then submit the business details and supporting documents requested by the Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office. Include the issued clearance with the rest of the permit checklist.

**Office:** Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office. **Charter:** pages 16-17.
