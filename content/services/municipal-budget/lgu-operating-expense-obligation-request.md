# LGU Operating Expense Obligation Request

The Municipal Budget Office processes obligation requests for LGU operating expenses.

## Requirements

### Procurement of goods and services

- Approved purchase request — 3 copies, signed by the end user and approved by the Local Chief Executive.
- Obligation request — 4 copies, signed by the requesting office head.

### Reimbursement of travel or training expenses

- Invitation letter — 3 copies.
- Approved travel order — 3 copies.
- Itinerary of travel (Appendix A) — 3 copies.
- Certificate of travel completed (Appendix B) — 4 copies.
- Certificate of appearance or participation — 1 original and 2 photocopies.
- Post-travel report — 3 copies.
- Obligation request — 4 copies, signed by the requesting office head.

## Process

1. Present the obligation request and complete attachments to the Budget Office, second floor of the Municipal Building.
2. The office checks the documents for completeness and records the amount granted in the registries of allotments and obligations.
3. Receive the signed obligation request and logbook entry; the office keeps a copy for filing.

The published process lists no fee. Confirm current requirements and processing time with the Municipal Budget Office.
