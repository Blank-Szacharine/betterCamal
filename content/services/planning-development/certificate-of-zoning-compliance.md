# Certificate of Zoning Compliance

The Certificate of Zoning Compliance confirms that a plan or proposed structure is properly zoned for its proposed use and meets the applicable zoning-ordinance provisions for the site.

## Basic requirements

The published MPDO presentation requests three copies of each document:

1. Duly completed and notarized application form.
2. Proof of the right over the land: a photocopy of the certificate of title and latest tax declaration. If the land is not registered in the applicant's name, submit the applicable notarized deed of sale, contract of lease, deed of donation, or authorization to use the land.
3. Vicinity map showing existing land uses within the prescribed radius from the project-site boundary.
4. Site development plan showing lot dimensions and the area of structures to be built, drawn to scale with measurements indicated.
5. Bill of materials, including quantities, unit costs, amounts, and estimated labor cost.
6. One set of building plans.
7. Zoning fee.

## Additional requirements for commercial, industrial, and institutional projects

- Applicable Environmental Clearance Certificate, Environmental Impact Assessment, or Ecological Site Assessment.
- For hospital projects, a Department of Health Permit to Construct where applicable.
- Affidavit of no objection from residents within a 100-meter radius.
- Barangay Council resolution endorsing the project, with meeting minutes.

Requirements and fees may change. Confirm the current checklist and application form with the Municipal Planning and Development Office before applying.
