# Mayor's Clearance - Camalaniugan

Request a mayor's clearance for job applications and related transactions.

## Prepare

Bring barangay clearance, MTC clearance, and police clearance. Submit the documents for verification and receive the signed clearance.

**Fee:** P100 plus P15 documentary stamp; free for first-time applicants. **Office:** Mayor's Office. **Charter:** page 8.
