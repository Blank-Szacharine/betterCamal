# Laboratory Program - Disease Control - Camalaniugan

This laboratory service supports disease-control programs through testing requested during consultation, screening, monitoring, or referral.

## Who this is for

Clients referred by the Municipal Health Office or enrolled in a disease-control program.

## What to prepare

- Health worker request, referral, or program record, when applicable
- Valid identification, when available
- Previous laboratory results and treatment records
- Follow any preparation instructions given before the test

## What to expect

1. Present the request or referral to the laboratory staff.
2. Complete the required specimen collection or laboratory procedure.
3. Receive instructions on results, follow-up, or referral.

Testing requirements, fees, and release times depend on the requested procedure. Confirm them with the Municipal Health Office before your visit.

## Office

**Municipal Health Office, Municipality of Camalaniugan**

This service appears in the Citizen's Charter under Laboratory Program - Disease Control, page 48.
