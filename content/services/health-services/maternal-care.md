# Maternal Care - Camalaniugan

Maternal Care includes scheduled prenatal check-ups, post-partum visits, and laboratory support for pregnant mothers.

## What to expect

1. Coordinate with the midwife assigned to your barangay for the weekly prenatal or post-partum visit.
2. Bring your maternal health record and any previous laboratory results, if available.
3. Attend the Rural Health Unit laboratory schedule arranged by catchment area.

The Municipal Health Office's published information says clients are notified one week before the visit. Confirm the current schedule and requirements with your midwife or the Municipal Health Office.

## Office

**Municipal Health Office, Municipality of Camalaniugan**
