# Expanded Program on Immunization - Camalaniugan

The Municipal Health Office schedules weekly barangay visits for immunization through the midwives assigned to each barangay.

## What to expect

1. Watch for the barangay schedule or notice from the assigned midwife.
2. Bring the child's health or immunization record, if available.
3. Attend the scheduled immunization visit and follow the health worker's instructions for the next dose or visit.

The published information says residents are notified one week before a visit. Confirm the current schedule and requirements with your barangay health worker or the Municipal Health Office.

## Office

**Municipal Health Office, Municipality of Camalaniugan**
