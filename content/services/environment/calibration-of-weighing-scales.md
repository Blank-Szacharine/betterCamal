# Calibration of Weighing Scales - Camalaniugan

Have weighing scales and commercial measurements checked for accurate readings.

Bring the equipment used in the business. The service checks and seals accurate instruments and issues a receipt. The charter lists P150 for a 10-kilogram scale, P300 for a 50-kilogram scale, and P1,400 for a baskula weighing scale. **Charter:** page 22.
