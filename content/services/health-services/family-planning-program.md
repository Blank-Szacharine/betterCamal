# Family Planning Program - Camalaniugan

The Family Planning Program provides counseling and access to appropriate family planning information and services through the Municipal Health Office.

## Who this is for

Individuals and couples who want guidance on family planning, birth spacing, or available methods.

## What to prepare

- Valid identification, when available
- Relevant medical history and current medicines
- Previous family planning or pregnancy records, if available
- A partner, parent, or guardian when required for the client’s situation

## What to expect

1. Register and request family planning counseling.
2. Discuss health history, preferences, and eligibility with health staff.
3. Receive counseling, the appropriate service, or a referral.

The health worker will explain the available method, follow-up schedule, and any warning signs. Confirm the current clinic schedule with the Municipal Health Office.

## Office

**Municipal Health Office, Municipality of Camalaniugan**

This service appears in the Citizen's Charter under Family Planning Program, page 46.
