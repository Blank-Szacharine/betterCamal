# Building, Electrical, and Other Permits - Camalaniugan

Apply for building, electrical, fencing, mechanical, demolition, sanitary, or occupancy permits.

Prepare five application forms, five signed and sealed plan sets, zoning clearance, Community Tax Certificate, TAN/TIN, official receipt, and Fire Safety Electrical Clearance. The process includes review, inspection, payment, evaluation, approval, and release.

**Fees:** National Building Code schedule. **Office:** Municipal Engineering Office. **Charter:** pages 15-16.
