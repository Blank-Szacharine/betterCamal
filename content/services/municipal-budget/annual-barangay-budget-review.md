# Annual Barangay Budget Review

The Municipal Budget Office reviews the annual budgets of Camalaniugan's barangays.

## Checklist

- Transmittal letter — Office of the Sangguniang Barangay.
- Appropriation ordinance — Office of the Sangguniang Barangay.
- Sangguniang-approved AIP — Office of the Sangguniang Barangay.
- List of projects chargeable against the 20% Development Fund — Office of the Punong Barangay.
- Plantilla of personnel — Office of the Punong Barangay.
- Statement of indebtedness, if any — Office of the Punong Barangay.
- DILG-endorsed GAD Plan and Budget — MLGOO.

Confirm the current checklist and submission schedule with the Municipal Budget Office before filing.
