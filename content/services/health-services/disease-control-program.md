# Disease Control Program - Camalaniugan

The Disease Control Program supports prevention, early detection, monitoring, treatment coordination, and referrals for community health concerns.

## Who this is for

Residents who need disease-control information, screening, treatment support, monitoring, or referral.

## What to prepare

- Valid identification, when available
- Symptoms, exposure history, and relevant dates
- Previous test results, prescriptions, or treatment records
- Contact information for follow-up

## What to expect

1. Register and describe the health concern to the health worker.
2. Complete the screening, consultation, or test requested by the program.
3. Receive health instructions, monitoring, treatment support, or referral.

Follow the instructions given by the health worker, especially for return visits and treatment completion. Confirm the current program schedule with the Municipal Health Office.

## Office

**Municipal Health Office, Municipality of Camalaniugan**

This service appears in the Citizen's Charter under Disease Control Program, page 47.
