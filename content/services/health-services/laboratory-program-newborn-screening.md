# Laboratory Program - Newborn Screening - Camalaniugan

Newborn screening helps identify certain conditions early so a baby can receive timely care and referral when needed.

## Who this is for

Newborn babies and their parents or guardians who need newborn screening assistance.

## What to prepare

- Newborn and parent or guardian information
- Birth or health facility record, when available
- Any instruction or referral provided by the birthing facility or health worker
- Contact details for result notification

## What to expect

1. Register the newborn with the health staff.
2. Follow the instructions for specimen collection and screening.
3. Receive instructions for results, follow-up, or referral.

Ask the Municipal Health Office about the recommended timing, available schedule, fee if applicable, and result release process.

## Office

**Municipal Health Office, Municipality of Camalaniugan**

This service appears in the Citizen's Charter under Laboratory Program Newborn Screening, page 49.
